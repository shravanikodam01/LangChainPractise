On a field where silence hums, then roars,
A leather ball flies, a bat implores.
Under the sun or stadium light,
Cricket dances from morn till night.

The bowler runs with measured grace,
Eyes locked firm on the batter’s face.
A swing, a seam, the crowd holds breath—
Each delivery whispers life or death.

The bat comes down with a thunderous sound,
The ball soars high, it kisses the ground.
Cheers erupt like a crashing sea,
In that moment—pure ecstasy.

From dusty lanes to grand arenas,
Dreams are stitched in whites and jerseys.
Every run a story told,
Of courage young and spirits bold.

In every match, a lesson lives—
Of patience, loss, and what one gives.
For cricket’s more than win or defeat,
It’s heart and soul in rhythm and beat. 🏏
